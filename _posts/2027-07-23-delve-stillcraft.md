I walk the same streets every day, and at some point they stop being interesting. Delve turns that walk into a descent. Under your district lies a dungeon, and its plan follows the streets above: a street is a corridor, a crossing is a hall, a block is solid rock. You light a torch for 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, step outside and go down. Somewhere on each floor are the stairs to the next one: the deeper, the more danger and the richer the loot.

It's free, with no ads and no account, and where you walk stays on your phone. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### Your streets, drawn as an old map

The streets come from OpenStreetMap and are drawn in ink, with hatched rock around the corridors and a blue grid underneath. Only what your torch lights is shown; walked corridors stay on the map. The town above shapes the rooms below: a church or graveyard becomes a crypt, a fountain a spring, a park a mushroom grotto, water an underground river, a shop a merchant.

### Fight it or walk it off

A creature in a hall can be fought: two or three blows, and the trick is to guard when it winds up, which opens it for a counter. Or you can simply walk on: after a couple of hundred metres it runs out of breath. Phone in the pocket, it buzzes at a hall, in a chase and at the stairs; small finds are picked up by themselves.

{% include figure.html path="blog/delve-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="A bat in the hall: fight, walk it off or offer a treat." %}

### The camp and a long way to grow

Every floor adds a quarter to the coins and materials you bring up – if you climb out alive through a well. If the torch dies, half the loot stays below. The loot builds a forge, an alchemist, a cartographer and three more buildings; beating the master of every fifth floor opens a deep camp to start from next time. Four classes, twenty levels with pairs of skills to choose from, gear with rarities, and daily guild orders.

### Eighty creatures

From a cellar rat in a thimble helmet to the heart of the town. Some can be tamed with a treat and will walk with you.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer, GPS.
- **Permissions:** location and a walk service with a quiet notification. The street map comes from OpenStreetMap; your position stays on the phone.
- **License:** MIT. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Allow location and make camp where you stand.
