I wanted a walking app where the walk itself is the food. In Roamling your town lies almost all under fog – the Veil, where streets nobody walks go dark – and you can only go out into it with a companion, a little creature that grows as you walk. The fog lifts where you pass, eggs turn up in it, and wild companions come by themselves, each in its own weather. The twist is that a companion grows into what your streets are like: walk a teen through parks and it grows leafy, along the river and it grows a watery form, through old yards, up hills, at night or in the rain – and it becomes something else.

It's free, with no ads and no account. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### 70 species, nearly 200 grown forms

Three starters to pick from, then woodland, water and town creatures, hill folk, night ones that come at dawn or under a full moon, weather ones that only appear in fog, a blizzard, the heat or at a rainbow's end, seasonal guests, and two legends. Each has a baby, a teen and two to four grown forms. The ones you haven't met yet show only as silhouettes.

### Fog over a real map

The GPS side comes from [Fogwalk](https://github.com/bigdevwhale/fogwalk): a filtered track, the fog cut away where you walk. The town is split into patches of about a hundred metres, and a patch you've never walked feeds the companions five times more than ordinary steps. OpenStreetMap tells parks, water, old streets and hills apart, so every patch has a taste – and every district on the map wears the colour of its kind.

{% include figure.html path="blog/roamling-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="The map: the fog, the districts by kind and today's scent." %}

### Where you walk is what they grow into

A teen keeps a diet of what it ate: green, water, stone, height, dusk and sky. Whatever leads when it grows up decides its form. A companion's card shows its world, its way from baby to the grown forms with the one it is heading for, and a passport: where it was born, how far you've walked together, its favourite weather.

{% include figure.html path="blog/roamling-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="A companion's card." %}

### Real weather

The weather comes from Open-Meteo for wherever you are. The sky over the rest stop is the one outside, every walk gets a stamp of its weather, and the wild companions of rain, fog, snow, heat and storms only come out then. After rain, if the sun comes through, a rainbow's end appears on the map for forty minutes.

{% include figure.html path="blog/roamling-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="The bestiary." %}

### A story

The town's districts are chapters. Each has five dwellers and a spirit asleep in the fog – a gardener, a miller otter, a street brownie, a belfry wind, a crossroads keeper, depending on what the district is like. Walk half of it, meet three of its dwellers there, and the spirit wakes, tells you its piece of the story and seals the Valley's Great Map. On the way come twelve letters from the First Walker, who once stopped going out – and whose companion stayed on the path and wrapped the town in fog to keep it.

{% include figure.html path="blog/roamling-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="The chronicle: districts, spirits and letters." %}

Every day the leader picks up a scent to a real place nearby – a fountain, an old tree, a bridge – with a find waiting there. There are three daily and three weekly tasks, a backpack for three with eggs warming in its pockets, a den under an old oak, and a little shop of rain gear and lanterns.

## Good to know

**Does it count all my steps?**

No – only the steps of a walk you started: from the phone's sensor, and if you turn it on, from your watch through Health Connect for that walk's time.

**What if I don't walk for a week?**

Nothing bad. Nobody dies or gets ill; a missed day is just a rest in the den, and the fog keeps the places you've walked.

**What goes online?**

Map tiles, the weather and OpenStreetMap queries, with rough coordinates and nothing about you. Walks, routes and companions stay on the phone.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer, GPS.
- **Permissions:** location, physical activity, notifications. No account, no ads, no analytics.
- **License:** MIT. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors. Fonts: Press Start 2P, Nunito, Inter and Caveat (OFL).

1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Allow location and pick your first companion.
