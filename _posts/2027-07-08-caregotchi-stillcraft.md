I wanted a tamagotchi that couldn't be fed by tapping a button for its sake – only by looking after myself. In Caregotchi a little pet lives in the hollow of an old tree and does everything with you: you have breakfast and it eats its porridge, you take a pill and it gets its berry, you go to bed and it curls up. Forget about yourself and it grows glum and catches a cold; a couple of good days put it back on its feet. The one rule I cared most about: being ill is never punished. When you write down a migraine, the pet loses nothing – it brings a blanket.

It's free, with no ads and no account. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### Five needs, five buttons

Food, water, medicine, moving and sleep are five rings around the pet, and five quick buttons below the hollow. After a meal one tap says how wholesome it was and whether there were vegetables – no calories. Sport is a kind and minutes; a walk to the shop counts too. None of it is strict: a day without sport is still half fine.

### A dark room for bad spells

«I feel bad» lists migraine, headache, stomach, back, anxiety, allergy, blood pressure, a cold and your own troubles. For a headache the screen dims to dark red with big buttons: strength on a ten-step scale, where it hurts on a drawn head, symptoms, what you took. Two hours later the pet asks «Feeling better?» right in the notification, and the answers slowly show which medicine works for you.

{% include figure.html path="blog/caregotchi-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="The dark room." %}

### Medicine on the windowsill

Each medicine grows in its own pot: taken on time it blooms, missed it wilts. Reminders come with a «Taken» button, there's a weekly pill box, the stock left in the pack, and a gentle count of painkiller days this month – taken too often, painkillers can cause headaches themselves.

{% include figure.html path="blog/caregotchi-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="The windowsill and the week's pill box." %}

### The detective and the diary

Once a few spells are written down, the detective compares days with spells and without – short nights, skipped breakfasts, little water, no moving, missed doses, pressure swings from the real weather – and pins notes like «3 of 4 spells came after a night shorter than 6 hours». You keep a note or strike it out. The month is a patchwork quilt with a cross-stitch for each spell, every day has its own page with the day's thread, the pet writes a letter on Sundays, and the doctor gets a three-month PDF.

{% include figure.html path="blog/caregotchi-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="The month as a quilt." %}

### The game around it

Twelve pets, each hatching from an egg earned by its own kind of care: a hedgehog nurse for thirty doses on time, an owlet for a week of good sleep, a phoenix for getting well after a cold. Levels, leaves to spend in the Badger's shop on things that appear in the hollow, three small tasks a day, a weekly chest for five good days, and sixteen embroidered badges.

{% include figure.html path="blog/caregotchi-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="The pets, the incubator and the badges." %}

## Good to know

**Can the pet die?**

No. It lives on what you do, not on how you feel. A blanket day freezes its needs and counts as good. There is an opt-in strict mode where, after a week without care, the pet leaves for grandma's in the village – and comes back after three days of care.

**Is it a medical app?**

No – it's a diary and a friend. It reminds, counts and looks for patterns; doses and treatment are for your doctor, and the PDF is there to help you talk to them.

**What goes online?**

Only your town's coordinates, for the weather and the air pressure. Everything else stays on the phone, and a single backup file moves it all to a new one.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** notifications, exact alarms, internet for the weather. No account, no ads, no analytics.
- **License:** MIT. Fonts: Nunito and Lora (OFL).

1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Pick your first pet and add your medicine – or leave it for later.
