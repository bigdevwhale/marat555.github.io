I read more when someone is waiting for me to. A small creature that grows on the pages I read turned out to be exactly that someone.

A reading tracker with a pixel-art familiar. Hatch an egg and read: it grows through five stages, from egg to ancient, and changes with what you read. A kitsune grows a tail for every genre, a dragon sits on a hoard of the books you finished. Neglect your books and it slowly turns to stone from the paws up, until a few pages break the spell. All the art is pixel art painted by the app itself. Free, no ads, no account.

## What's inside

### Eight familiars

Mimic, kitsune or slime hatch at the start; the dragon, phoenix, griffin, owlbear and moon moth are found by reading: a 400-page book, five late nights, seven days in a row. Each loves its own kind of reading, and about one egg in sixteen hatches shining, in a rare colouring.

### It grows with what you read

Five stages from egg to ancient. The kitsune grows a tail in each finished genre's colour, the slime swallows a thing you name after a chapter, the owlbear puts on spectacles, the moth gets a star for every night of reading. After horror it trembles, after romance it blushes.

### The den

A pixel room furnished with sparks, one for every mana ever eaten: a window with the real day or night, a bookshelf in the colours of your books, a bubbling cauldron, a mandrake peeking from its pot. Stroke the familiar to make it purr, and after midnight it sleeps.

### The ritual

A candle burns down while you read: turn pages with a rustle on screen, or just say where you stopped at the end. A fireplace or rain, and with the phone face down the familiar dozes on your lap. Switch any book to its audio version and a gramophone counts the minutes; or log the page or just the minutes later.

{% include figure.html path="blog/familiar-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="The feast. What it ate after a sitting." %}

### Find a book

Search Open Library, with Google Books as a fallback, by title or author: the author, the pages and the real cover fill in, and the cover is kept on the phone. Or pick your own picture, or get a cover drawn in the genre's colour. Make your own shelves too.

### Cycles, the tree and the saga

Books gather into cycles with a reward for reading them all, every finished book grows a glowing fruit on the tree of stories, and a goal for the year shows your pace. The saga tells your year as a legend with a square for every day of reading. Three monthly guild errands, an evening line in the familiar's voice and a home-screen widget.

## Good to know

**What if I stop reading for a while?**

After two quiet days the familiar starts turning to stone, from the paws up. Nothing is lost: a few pages crack the spell. The phoenix never turns to stone; it burns to ash and rises again when you come back.

**Does it need the internet?**

Only to search for a book and download its cover. Books, sittings and familiars stay on the phone. No account, no ads, no analytics.

**Does it count audiobooks and e-books?**

Yes. Audiobooks are counted in minutes with a gramophone instead of a candle. Read on a train or in another app? Just tell it where you stopped and it eats those pages too.

**What if I change phones?**

Settings → Backup saves your books, sittings and familiars into one JSON file. Restore it on the new phone.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer. No Google services needed.
- **Permissions:** Internet only to search a book and download its cover. Notifications for the evening reminder. Your reading stays on the phone.
- **License:** MIT

1. Download the APK on your phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Choose an egg, add the book you're reading and light the first candle.
