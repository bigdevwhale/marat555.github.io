In December I like looking at the windows across the yard. One lights up, then another; someone puts up a tree, someone has a lamp with a green shade. I wanted an advent calendar like that: not a grid of little doors, but a house where one window lights each evening.

The House Opposite is that calendar. A five-storey house stands across the yard with 24 dark windows. Each evening has a small real deed, and once you've done it, one window lights up after the real sunset in your town. Behind it is a room with its people, and somewhere in the house someone is leaving presents at the doors. It's free, with no ads, no accounts and no internet. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### A window at sunset

Every evening has a "spark", a small deed from real life: call someone you've been meaning to call, step into the yard after dark and stand there for five minutes. Do it, and the window lights, but only after the sun has really set where you are. The app works the sunset out from your rough location, or from the time zone if you'd rather not share it. Tomorrow's window can't be opened today, however hard you tap.

Missed an evening? There are no streaks to lose. Light the window later with a candle; a spark on a Sunday earns one.

### Rooms with people

Each lit window opens into a room drawn in code: wallpaper, a rug, a lamp, a tree in the corner, the people who live there. Lev Markovich the pianist at his piano. Arkady, who drives a taxi at night and whose tea has gone cold for the third time. Maya, whose cat Bagel ran away last night and came back in the morning, fed and pleased with himself. Tap the things in the room to read notes, find the day's clue and, sometimes, a hidden mandarin.

{% include figure.html path="blog/opposite-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="A room behind a lit window." %}

### The Secret Grandfather

Someone in the house leaves presents at the doors, and nobody has seen who. There are ten suspects, and each has six habits: handwriting, wrapping, footprints, the hour, gait and smell. Every room gives a clue: "An unsigned card on the windowsill. The handwriting is neat, every letter in place." Early clues are vague and fit several people; they sharpen slowly, so the answer only becomes certain around the 21st–23rd.

The notebook collects the clues on its ruled lines, and each suspect has a file where their habits turn green or get struck out against what you've found. On the 24th you name the culprit. If you're wrong, the notebook shows which clue doesn't fit. The culprit and the clues change from season to season, and each of the ten has their own ending.

### The tree

Every lit window hangs a glass ornament on your tree: a ball, an icicle, a rocket, a little house, a bell. A found mandarin makes it a "granny's" ornament with a gold cap. The tree itself is grown in code branch by branch, with needles, tinsel, silver rain, a garland of coloured bulbs, a red spire and cotton-wool Grandfather Frost and Snow Maiden underneath. When the season ends, it goes up to the shelf with the trees of past years.

{% include figure.html path="blog/opposite-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="An ornament for every window." %}

### Real presents, if you like

As in an ordinary advent calendar, any window can hide a real present: something to find, a treat, a note or a deed. A bow marks the window, and the present shows in the room once it's lit. If you're making the calendar for someone dear, switch to "for someone else": the list is sealed so they can't peek, and it says who it's from.

## Good to know

**Can I play it outside December?**

Yes. When you start, choose "tonight", and the 24 windows go one evening at a time from today.

**What if I miss an evening?**

The window waits. Light it later with a candle.

**It's the first version. What if something breaks?**

Please tell me: Settings has buttons to report a bug or suggest an idea through the StillCraft bot.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** notifications for the sunset reminder and approximate location for the sunset time, both optional. No internet permission at all.
- **License:** MIT. Fonts: Lora and Caveat.

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Choose to start on December 1 or tonight, and wait for the sunset.
