Solitaire is one of the few games I can play without thinking about anything else, but most solitaire apps are anything but calm: timers, scores, ads between deals. Patience is the opposite. Six classic games, each with its own watercolour deck and its own table – Klondike on a veranda by a rainy lake, Spider in a forest glade, FreeCell in a tea garden – no timer, no score, and any move can be taken back. An old owl called Agatha teaches every game and shows you the next move if you get stuck.

It's free, with no ads, no account and no internet. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### Six games, six decks, six tables

Klondike (draw one or three), Spider (one, two or four suits), FreeCell, Pyramid, TriPeaks and Golf. Every game has a deck of its own: herons, foxes, hares and owls for Klondike, badgers, deer, hedgehogs and ravens for Spider, cranes and red pandas for FreeCell, fennec foxes and camels for Pyramid. The table behind the cards is a painted landscape, and three more – an attic, a night train and a lighthouse – open with time.

### An owl who teaches

The first time you open a game, Agatha shows its rules on a few pages drawn with real cards. Then she sits down to play with you: for the first moves she lights up the cards, points where they go and says why. After that you're on your own, but the lantern on the right is always there – tap it and the same arrow and note come back.

### Deals that come out

A solver checks the deals in advance, so every Klondike, FreeCell, Pyramid, TriPeaks, Golf and one-suit Spider deal can be finished. Moves can be taken back as many times as you like, and when nothing is left to do the owl suggests going back to the fork.

{% include figure.html path="blog/patience-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="Pyramid: pairs that make thirteen, in an oasis at sunset." %}

### An album, guests and a flame

Every finished game adds one brush stroke to a watercolour picture in the album: twelve pictures, three for each season. Twelve animals and birds come to the table by themselves – the cat for your first finished game, the moth late at night, the hedgehog when you play without hints. There's one Klondike deal a day, the same for everyone, and a candle on the table that burns bright on the days you played and never goes out.

## Good to know

**Can every deal be finished?**

In Klondike, FreeCell, Pyramid, TriPeaks, Golf and one-suit Spider, yes. Spider with two and four suits is not fully checked yet.

**Why landscape only?**

Seven Klondike columns and ten Spider columns fit on a wide table with the cards still large.

**What goes online?**

Nothing. Games in progress, the album and the guests stay on the phone.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** none. No account, no ads, no analytics.
- **License:** MIT. Font: Kurale (OFL).

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Turn the phone sideways and open the Klondike box – Agatha will show you how it goes.
