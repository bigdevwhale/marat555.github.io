Familiar raises a pet on the pages you read, Songling on the music you play. Keyling is for the other side of books: writing them. You log the words you wrote, and the residents of your keyboard eat them, grow and slowly furnish your desk.

It's meant for anyone with a long text on the go – a novel, stories, a thesis. Keyling doesn't read your text; it only counts. It's free, with no ads, no account and no network access. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### The desk

The home screen is a writing desk seen from above: a notebook, a steaming mug, a plant and a mechanical keyboard. The space bar is propped up on two matchsticks, and in the nook under it, among fairy lights and a stack of tiny books, lives a dormouse called Spacelet.

Log the words you've added – or type the total from Word or Google Docs and let Keyling work out what's new – and letters rain onto the keys. Spacelet gathers them up and munches. Typo bugs crawl between the rows: "its/it's", a stray comma, "the the". Catch them with a finger and they go in a jar. A week away and dust settles on the keyboard; fifty words blow it off.

### Seven key residents

Seven creatures live under seven keys, and each eats its own part of the writing work: Spacelet eats words, a heron in a bow tie under Enter comes to a feast for every finished chapter, an eraser in a paper sleeve under Backspace eats editing, a frog under Caps Lock sings through sprints, an inchworm in glasses under Tab loves a book plan, an owl in a night cap under Shift eats nights after midnight, and a snail with a suitcase under Esc comes on an honest day off.

{% include figure.html path="blog/keyling-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="Tap a key on the map and its cap lifts: who lives there, what it eats and how to feed it now." %}

Each grows through levels – baby, youngster, grown, ancient – and visibly gets bigger. One of them lives on the desk at a time, and while it's there its ability works: more coins for chapters, double pay for editing, a gentler daily goal, a spare day that keeps your streak.

### Sprints and the ghost

Pick 10, 15, 25 or 45 minutes and write wherever you like. The ghost is your best sprint so far; at the end you see whether you beat it.

### Totals

A year goal with a pace marker and a forecast for 31 December, a calendar of writing days, months, hours and weekdays. Books show author's sheets, pages, chapters, hours of editing and a finish date, and are measured against the classics. Records keep your best day, week, month, streak, sprint and latest word, and there are nearly forty awards, a few of them secret.

{% include figure.html path="blog/keyling-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="The year: a goal, the pace, a calendar of writing days." %}

{% include figure.html path="blog/keyling-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="Awards, from the first word to a hundred hours of editing." %}

Any of it can go out as a card – the year, a month, a week, a book, a streak, a calendar, records, a resident or an award. You see the card first, then share or save it.

### The workshop

Words, daily goals, chapters and awards earn coins, and coins furnish the desk: dark walnut or green baize, an enamel mug or a tea glass in a holder, a monstera, a banker's lamp, a candle, a globe, a sleeping cat. Everything can be tried on first, and things on the desk can be dragged around. Your own mug can be made from any photo.

{% include figure.html path="blog/keyling-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="Trying things on before buying them." %}

Keycap sets change the keyboard, and the caps wear the way real ones do: O, E, A and the space bar first. The key sounds are recordings of three real switches, and every key in the app clicks.

## Good to know

**Does it count words by itself?**

No. Keyling never sees your text: you type how many words you added, or the total from your editor, and it works out the rest.

**Words or characters?**

Either. Settings switch to characters with spaces, and the stats also speak in author's sheets and pages.

**It's the first version. What if something breaks?**

Please tell me: Workshop → settings has buttons to report a bug or suggest an idea through the StillCraft bot.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** none – no internet, no account.
- **License:** MIT. Fonts: Press Start 2P, Lora and Inter (OFL). Key sounds from kbsim (MIT).

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Name your book and log the first words.
