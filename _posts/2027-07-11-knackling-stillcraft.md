Most of us have a hobby we keep meaning to get back to: the guitar in the corner, the embroidery hoop in a drawer, the sketchbook with three pages filled. I wanted a reason to pick them up that wasn't guilt. In Knackling every hobby has its own little apprentice living in a cosy attic workshop – a kitten with a hoop for embroidery, a fox cub with a fretboard for a tail for guitar, an owl with a ribbon bookmark for reading. It hatches from an egg at your first session and grows as you practise, all the way to a legend that takes ten thousand hours.

It's free, with no ads, no account and no internet. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### Forty hobbies, fifty-five apprentices

Music, drawing and watercolour, embroidery, knitting and crochet, pottery and woodwork, reading, writing and languages, chess, gardening, fishing, birdwatching, dance, yoga, cycling, cooking, baking, coding, electronics, astronomy – forty in all, each with a creature drawn for it. If yours isn't there, fifteen free apprentices take any hobby you name, with whatever you want to count besides minutes.

### Ten ranks to ten thousand hours

Hatchling at the first session, Pupil at three hours, Apprentice at fifteen, Master at fifty, Grand master at a hundred and fifty, and then Guild master, Magister, Virtuoso, Grandmaster and Legend at ten thousand hours. From five hundred hours on, the apprentice wears a golden glow. Photos, notes, finished projects and the daily challenge add a little extra.

{% include figure.html path="blog/knackling-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="A hobby: the rank, the road to ten thousand hours, the challenge of the day." %}

### A session in two taps

The red pincushion at the bottom starts the timer; it keeps ticking in the notification shade with the app closed. Or log a session afterwards. Either way you can add how much you did (pages, rows, songs, kilometres), how it went, a photo of the work, a note and a project.

{% include figure.html path="blog/knackling-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="Logging a session." %}

### The ledger

The year is a patchwork quilt where every day takes the colour of the hobby that got most of your time. Hours per hobby are drawn as skeins of yarn; a board shows which weekdays and times of day you actually practise; twelve weeks stack up against your goals.

{% include figure.html path="blog/knackling-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="The month as a quilt and skeins of hours." %}

### The game around it

Every apprentice suggests a small challenge each day – 160 of them, like «play your favourite song at half speed». There are daily and weekly tasks with chests, sparks to spend on things for the workshop, a shine elixir, projects with a showcase wall and more than eighty embroidered patches for the apron. And postcards: an apprentice, a finished work, your month or year, as a post or a story.

{% include figure.html path="blog/knackling-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="A postcard of the month." %}

## Good to know

**Can an apprentice die?**

No. Leave a hobby for a while and its apprentice gets bored, then dozes off. Come back and it wakes up with its rank intact.

**Does it work with Lingvale?**

Yes. Knackling's workshop joins the Lingvale village next to the other StillCraft pets.

**What goes online?**

Nothing. Sessions, notes and photos stay on the phone, and one backup file, photos included, moves it all to a new one.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** notifications, for a running session's clock and a gentle nudge. No account, no ads, no analytics.
- **License:** MIT. Fonts: Nunito and Caveat (OFL).

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Pick your first hobby – the egg hatches at your first session.
