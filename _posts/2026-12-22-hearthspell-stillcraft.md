Housework is the most repeatable quest there is. Hearthspell turns your home into a wizard's tower, one floor per room, in hand-painted storybook art.

The old master left his tower to you, his apprentice. The tower is your home: every floor is a room, every chore is a spell that comes round in its own time. Cast it and the floor shines; forget it and harmless imps move in. Master a spell and its helper wakes up, a broom or a teapot that paces the floor. Mana buys decor, the master writes once a week, the shopping list becomes a quest for reagents, and a chronicle shows how your week went. Postcards share the tower with friends. Hand-painted art, no streaks to lose, no ads, no account, no internet access.

## What's inside

### Floors are rooms

Eleven kinds of room, each a detailed painted scene: the kitchen is the Alchemy Lab, the bedroom an Observatory under a domed window, the bathroom the Spring Baths, the hallway the Gate Hall, the balcony a Greenhouse. Rename, reorder and add floors.

### Spells in their own time

45 ready-made chores with sensible intervals, from the dishes every day to the oven once a moon, each with a small incantation, or your own. Freshness fades slowly instead of a checklist that resets every morning.

{% include figure.html path="blog/hearthspell-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="Floors. Helpers at work, imps moving in." %}

### Imps, not punishment

An overdue spell brings a cheeky imp to its floor. It chews socks and giggles, nothing burns down, and one cast sweeps it out. A dusty floor just waits for you.

### Helpers wake up

17 helpers, from the Broom and the Sponge to the Teapot and the Satchel. Cast a spell five times and its helper wakes and starts pacing its floor, then grows through four levels.

{% include figure.html path="blog/hearthspell-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="A floor. Its spells and their freshness." %}

### Mana, decor and the master's requests

Every cast earns mana for 20 pieces of decor, from a cat to a fireplace. Each week the master asks for the three spells that waited longest, worth double until Sunday, and twelve letters tell the story of his travels.

### A market of reagents

The shopping list sorted into shop sections with tower names, the section guessed from the name. It learns how often you buy things, suggests what's running low, and market mode is a big one-hand checklist.

{% include figure.html path="blog/hearthspell-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="Grimoire. Spells by floor, helpers and collections." %}

### A chronicle of your week

This week against the last, a chart of the last fourteen days, how tidy each floor is, your favourite spells and the totals since the tower became yours. Sunday tea still tells the week in a few warm lines; the chronicle has the numbers.

{% include figure.html path="blog/hearthspell-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="Chronicle. The week, a chart and the tidiest floors." %}

### Postcards

Your week, the tower, the apprentice, any room, a helper, an imp you've shown the door or the treasure shelf, as a pretty card with a QR code to the app. Share it anywhere or save it to the gallery.

{% include figure.html path="blog/hearthspell-post/shot-5.jpg" caption="Postcards. Every one carries a QR code to Google Play." %}

## Good to know

**What if I skip a few days?**

Nothing breaks. There are no streaks to lose. Spells fade, imps move in, and the next cast sweeps them out. The master never scolds.

**Can I add my own chores?**

Yes. Any name, any interval from a day to a year, three levels of effort and the helper it wakes. You can pause a spell, or every spell at once in holiday mode while you're away.

**Is it for a family?**

This version is one tower on one phone. A shared tower for the whole household is planned.

**What if I change phones?**

Settings → Backup saves the whole tower, every cast and the shopping list into one JSON file. Restore it on the new phone.
