Most habit apps are a counter: days without a cigarette, money saved, a streak that turns to zero the evening you slip. I wanted something kinder. In Wildling every bad habit is a wild beast from the thicket. It comes for its due, you don't feed it, and day by day it comes closer: a shadow between the trees, eyes at the forest edge, by the fence, on the porch, and at last asleep by the hearth. Good habits are here too, as shy creatures that step out of the grass the more often you do them. A slip doesn't send you back to the start; the beast only takes a step back.

It's free, with no ads and no account, and it doesn't even ask for internet access. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### 48 creatures

Twelve wild beasts for ready habits – a smoke dragon for cigarettes, a foam-maned bog bear for alcohol, a sticky candy lizard for sweets, a moth with screen-panel wings for scrolling in bed – and twelve shy ones for good habits: a moss creature with twig antlers for walks, a water sprite for drinking enough, a cloud lamb for going to bed on time. Eleven more of each wait for habits you name yourself, and four rare guests come on their own: for holding a week after a slip, a craving held deep in the night, a hundred days, ten cravings.

### The yard and the house

The home screen is a lodge at the forest's edge in two views. The yard has the thicket, the fence, three garden beds and the porch; the house, cut open, has a kitchen, a living room with a hearth, a bedroom and an attic with a telescope. Every creature stands where its trust has brought it. Below, today is split in two: the wild ones not to feed, with their clean days, and the shy ones to lure, with a circle to tick.

### «It came»

The lantern in the middle of the menu is for the moment it pulls. The beast comes to the night window and you sit with it for four minutes: breathe in and out, a simple thing to do, your own reason, and a letter you wrote yourself on a good day. A craving is a wave; it rises and falls. At the end you write one line about what helped.

{% include figure.html path="blog/wildling-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="It came: four minutes by the window." %}

### The journal trail

A few words a day, with the weather inside instead of an emoji – clear, sun, a feather on the wind, fog, falling leaves, a storm, rain, the moon behind clouds. The pages hang on a trail through the forest with the day number, a stamp of how your creature looked that day, and milestones like «A week» and «A month» on the way.

{% include figure.html path="blog/wildling-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="The journal: the weather of four weeks and the trail." %}

### The map of hard days

From the gate of day one to the lodge on the hill a year on, past a campfire, thorns under a storm on day three, a swamp on day ten, a roadside tavern of temptation at three weeks. Each place says what usually happens then, so a bad day is expected rather than a verdict. The places you haven't reached yet stay grey.

{% include figure.html path="blog/wildling-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="The map of hard days." %}

### The chronicle

Money and time won back, and what that would be in films and books; each creature's lane to the hearth; records; this month against the last; the hardest day of the week; and how the weather inside goes with your habits. The cravings get their own corner: the beast's clock with the hours it comes, the sea of the month's waves, what brings it and the notes of what helps.

{% include figure.html path="blog/wildling-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="The chronicle." %}

Pinecones come for clean days, habits done and cravings sat out, and buy seeds for the beds and things for the yard. Five kinds of postcards – a creature, the chronicle, a milestone, the weather inside, the lodge – are there to share.

## Good to know

**What happens when I slip?**

Tap «slipped?». The beast takes a step back, a fifth of its trust and no more. The way you've come stays, and so does everything in the garden.

**Can I track something that isn't in the list?**

Yes. Pick one of the creatures for your own habits and give the habit its name.

**Does it go online?**

No. The app has no internet permission at all: habits, notes and creatures stay on the phone.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** notifications only, for the evening question. No account, no ads, no analytics.
- **License:** MIT. Fonts: Press Start 2P, Nunito, Inter and Caveat (OFL).

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Pick a habit and the creature for it.
