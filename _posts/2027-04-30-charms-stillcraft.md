Every family knows the evening loop: «teeth», «teeth!», «I said teeth!». Chore charts with stickers work for a week, and then the grown-up is the reminder again. I wanted the reminding to move into the app and the child to have a reason to beat it.

Charms is a grimoire for a young sorcerer. Every deed of the day is a page with its own magic hour; a picture of the deed done turns into an engraving, in gold if the child got there before the page lit up. It's free, with no ads and no accounts, and it has no internet permission at all.

## What's inside

### Gold, silver and ink

Make the bed by eight, brush teeth by ten past. Before its hour a page is quiet, and a charm cast then comes out in gold. At the hour the page starts to glow and a quiet notification comes, so the app reminds instead of you; from then on it's silver. If a grown-up still has to remind, they don't raise their voice: they ring a bell on the mentor's page, the child's phone goes «ding», and the charm comes out in ink. It still counts.

{% include figure.html path="blog/charms-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="Today: some pages done, some glowing." %}

### The engraving

The child photographs the made bed, the empty plate or the open exercise book with the phone's own camera. The app never opens the camera itself. On the phone the picture is engraved: light becomes hatching in gold, silver or ink, crossed where it's brightest, with fine outlines along the edges. A frame carries the deed's name between two sigils, the date and the deed's patron creature. The photo itself is deleted straight away. If you'd rather keep photographs, the mentor turns engravings off and the picture stays a colour photo in the same frame.

### Spells and the cauldron

When every page of a part of the day is charmed, a spell is cast: Dawn, Noon, Dusk or Moon Sleep. A week of charms fills the cauldron, with three drops for gold, two for silver and one for ink. On Sunday it brews a small, middling or great potion, and the child picks one from the shelf. Every potion is a real reward the family chose together: dinner of your choice, one more story at bedtime, half an hour when nobody asks for anything, a trip to the funfair.

{% include figure.html path="blog/charms-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="A full week, ready to brew." %}

### The bestiary

Eight creatures from the tales look after the deeds: the Midnight Owl keeps the homework, Bayun the Cat the made bed, the Tidy Fox the teeth, the Lamplighter Firefly lights out. After three golden charms of a deed in a row, its patron comes to visit and stands right on the engraving.

{% include figure.html path="blog/charms-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="Who has come to visit." %}

### Seals

Some deeds can't be photographed. Lights out is sealed by holding a finger on the page until the ring closes, and it finishes the Moon Sleep spell.

{% include figure.html path="blog/charms-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="Lights out, sealed in gold." %}

### The mentor's page

Four digits only the grown-up knows. There you set the deeds, their hours and days, fill the potion shelf, ring the bell, put a blot on a picture that isn't the deed (its drops leave the cauldron), give out brewed potions and mark a day of rest when someone is ill. A day of rest asks nothing and loses nothing. There are no crosses and no lost streaks anywhere.

{% include figure.html path="blog/charms-post/shot-5.jpg" caption="The bell and the blot." %}

## Good to know

**Does the child need their own phone?**

No. The grimoire lives on one phone: the child's own or a family one that stays at home. The mentor's page opens on the same phone.

**What if the picture isn't the deed?**

The mentor puts a blot on it. The page fades and its drops leave the cauldron, and that's all.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 8 (API 26) or newer.
- **Permissions:** notifications, so pages light up at their hour. No internet, no camera permission.
- **License:** MIT. Fonts: Cormorant, Manrope.

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Set up the grimoire together: the sorcerer's name, the deeds and the mentor's four digits.
