Breathing apps tell you to breathe in for four and out for six, and after a week you forget they exist. Puffling gives the count a body: a puffin chick in a little tea house on a sea cliff, which puffs up as you breathe in and settles as you breathe out. Practices feed it and its friends calm, and they grow at night while they sleep.

It's free, with no ads and no account. Every sound is made inside the app, and the only thing that leaves the phone is your town's coordinates, so the round window can show the real sky. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### The tea house

The home screen is a wooden tea house with a big round window. Through it you see the sky and the weather in your town, a lighthouse on a rock and the sea – which rises towards high tide with every minute you practise this week. Below are the tide, what to do right now, a few kind words for the day, and three small tiles for the bell, the diary and the week's quests. A «Rough sea» button sits on the scene for a hard moment: three sighs of relief and grounding.

### Breathing with a pet

The pet is the pacer. It fills out on the in-breath and settles on the out-breath, a soft whoosh follows each breath and the phone taps lightly at every turn, so you can close your eyes. There are eight techniques – box, 4-7-8, coherent breathing, the physiological sigh, the long exhale, alternate nostril and more. Lie down with the phone on your belly and it feels your real breath and counts breaths a minute.

### Wim Hof as a dive

The Wim Hof rounds come from SwissZen: four levels with hold targets and your own pace. The hold is where Puffling differs – while you don't breathe, the pet dives, half a metre a second. A starfish at fifteen seconds, a ray at a minute and a quarter, an anglerfish with its little lamp further down. Everything it sees goes into the aquarium.

### Stillness, the body, the bell

Silent sitting with bells, a body scan, walking meditation, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a cup of tea drunk slowly, a candle flame. A few times a day a bell rings at a random moment – stop for three breaths, and a lantern lights up on the path. It keeps quiet during calls and in silent mode.

### Kind words and a diary

120 affirmations in eight themes, plus your own. Say one aloud, the phone hears it, and the phrase sails off in a bottle that washes back a few weeks later. The diary is for feelings: tap a colour – joy, calm, sadness, anxiety, anger, tiredness – pick the exact word, add what it was about and a few lines. Three pets are found only through the diary.

{% include figure.html path="blog/puffling-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="The diary: a feeling in two taps." %}

### Pets and the summary

Twenty-three pets, each with an ability: the sloth loves slow breathing, the pufferfish long holds, the owl evening practice, the chameleon the diary. The summary shows the minutes, a mood map by day, and – if you mark how it felt before and after – which practices calm you best.

## Good to know

**I've never meditated. Is it for me?**

Yes. The round button asks what you need right now and offers a practice of a couple of minutes. The «A week of breathing» course goes step by step.

**Is Wim Hof safe?**

Only sitting or lying down – never in water, in the bath or at the wheel. Light-headedness and tingling are normal, but don't push through. Not with heart conditions, epilepsy or in pregnancy.

**It's the first version. What if something breaks?**

Please tell me: Settings → More has buttons to report a bug or suggest an idea through the StillCraft bot.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** internet only for the weather in the window; notifications for the bell. No microphone permission, no account, no ads, no analytics.
- **License:** MIT. Fonts: Nunito, Inter and Press Start 2P (OFL). Weather by Open-Meteo and MET Norway.
