Everyone knows the 20-20-20 rule, and almost nobody follows it: the reminder pops up, you swipe it away and keep reading. Second Sight wraps the same break in a small story. High in the mountains lives an old seer, and you are her apprentice. While you look at a screen, her crystal orb slowly fills with mist; when it clouds over, she calls you to the window. Twenty seconds of looking far away and the orb is clear again, with a glimpse left inside. Six glimpses and a minute in the dark under your palms, and the orb shows you a vision from a tale.

It's free, with no ads, no account and no internet at all. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### The orb mists with screen time

The app counts how long the screen has been on. While the phone lies still, your eyes rest and the mist clears by itself. If you work at a computer rather than on the phone, turn on «I'm at the computer» and the orb mists by the clock instead. The reminder is one tap: «Look far» in the notification and the exercise is already running. It stays quiet during calls, at night and while the app is open.

### Twelve practices, most with your eyes closed

Looking far; «Eight directions» and «Two lanterns», where the gaze goes as far as it can every way; «Dew» for blinking; near and far; side vision; and «Darkness», the old palming with warm hands over closed eyes. For the body there are hands, neck and shoulders, and a slow breath. The seer says aloud what to do, and a bell and a buzz mark every change, so you don't have to watch the screen. In the rituals the next exercise starts by itself.

### The apprentice's path

A 21-day course, one lesson a day of a few minutes: first just learning to rest, then shifting focus, then moving the eyes, then the wide gaze. Missing a day takes nothing away; you simply carry on. At the end the seer gives you a gift.

{% include figure.html path="blog/secondsight-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="Before a practice: what, why and how." %}

### Tales and pilgrims

There are six tales of eight round paintings each: a star that fell into a lotus pond, a boatman and his crane, a badger who grows tea, a potter who mends the moon with gold. Every day a pilgrim climbs up to the pavilion with a question, and the orb answers once you've done the practices it asks for. And every 15 minutes in daylight bring a bunch of a seasonal tea herb; the light sensor notices when you're outdoors.

## Good to know

**Will it improve my eyesight?**

No, and no app can honestly promise that. It helps your eyes tire less: regular breaks, blinking and more daylight. If your sight is getting worse, see an eye doctor.

**Reminders don't arrive.**

On Xiaomi, Huawei and some other phones, let the app run in the background and exempt it from battery saving. The settings have buttons for both.
