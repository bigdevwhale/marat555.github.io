We usually pick cafés by stars and reviews on a map, but the ones I care about are the three or four I keep going back to. I wanted somewhere to keep them, so I built a notebook for them.

A diary of the cafés you keep coming back to. Every café gets a page in a notebook and a sugar packet pinned to a cork board under string lights. Every cup leaves a coffee ring on the page, and the rings pile up where your cup always lands. From the tenth visit the café is yours, and the app asks the barista's question: the usual? Free, no ads, no account.

## What's inside

### A page for every café

The name and address, the front, the stamps (sockets, quiet, laptop OK, open late…) and a line of its story. The front is drawn from memory in the packet's colours, or it's your own photo, as it is or redrawn as an ink sketch.

### A sugar packet on the board

Every café gets a packet in one of five prints and ten colourways, pinned to the cork board with a paper tag: "yours · 27", "8 visits", "from Anya", "2019–2026".

### A coffee ring for every cup

"I'm in a café" writes down a cup. Its ring goes on the page in the colour of the drink, and cups of the usual pile up in the same spot. Beside a ring there's room for a pencil line: who was there, what happened.

{% include figure.html path="blog/theusual-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="Visit 28. One tap: yes, the usual." %}

### The usual?

Write it down, or just order the same thing twice and it writes itself. From the tenth visit the café is stamped *yours*, and when you walk in, one tap answers "Yes, the usual".

### An hour by the window

Put the phone face down and look out. Time counts only while it lies face down, and goes onto the cup.

### Packets for friends

Send a café as a sugar packet with a sticky note: "get the filter, sit on the windowsill". It goes as plain text through any messenger. A friend with The Usual shares it back into the app, and it lands on their board as a dashed "want to go" packet.

### When a café closes

Its page stays, with every ring, and the window on it goes dark: chairs up on the table, a sign on the door and your last line. The packet gets a black ribbon.

{% include figure.html path="blog/theusual-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="Closed. The page stays." %}

### The year in cups

A long till receipt: cups, cafés, the new ones, hours by the window, the most faithful place, the drink and the company of the year, the closed ones, and your own price index for the drink you order most.

{% include figure.html path="blog/theusual-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="The year in cups, and what a raf cost." %}

### The map

Your cafés as little sugar packets on an OpenStreetMap plan printed in sepia, one city at a time. Put a café there with "I'm here" or by moving the map under the pin. A packet sent to a friend carries its place too.

{% include figure.html path="blog/theusual-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="The map. Sepia OpenStreetMap, by city." %}

## How a café becomes yours

| | On the board | On the page |
|---|---|---|
| **A friend's tip** | A dashed packet: "from Anya" | Their sticky note: what to order, where to sit |
| **First visit** | A new packet is pinned up | The front, the address, the first ring |
| **Coming back** | The tag counts the visits | Rings pile up at your spot; the same order twice becomes the usual |
| **Tenth visit** | "yours · 10" | The stamp, and "The usual?" as you walk in |
| **It closes** | A black ribbon and the years | The window goes dark; every ring and your last line stay |

## Good to know

**Does it need the internet?**

Only for the map. Its tiles come from OpenStreetMap, and opening the map picker asks Nominatim once for the address. The diary itself works offline and stays on the phone: no account, no ads, no analytics.

**Does it know where I am?**

Only when you tap "I'm here" to put a café on the map. Nothing runs in the background, and you can always place a café by moving the map under the pin instead.

**How does a friend get my packet?**

As plain text through any messenger, so anyone can read it. A friend with The Usual shares the text back into the app, and the café lands on their board as a "want to go" packet, with your note and its place on the map.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9.0 (API 28) or newer. No Google services needed.
- **Permissions:** Internet only for OpenStreetMap map tiles and one Nominatim address lookup when the map picker opens. Location only when you tap "I'm here". No camera permission: the phone's own camera app takes the photos.
- **License:** MIT

1. Download the APK on your phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Next time you sit down with a cup, tap "I'm in a café".
