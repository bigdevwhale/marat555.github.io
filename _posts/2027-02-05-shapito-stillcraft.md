I put off one phone call for three weeks once. When I finally made it, it took four minutes and was perfectly pleasant. Most of my fears work like that: the dread is big, the thing itself is small. People grow by stepping just past the edge of comfort — a little, often, and with a way back — so I wanted a way to do that every day without it feeling like homework.

Big Top turns what you find hard into eight circus acts. Speaking in public is the ringmaster's turn, saying no is the lion tamer's, the unknown is the tightrope, mistakes are the juggler's. Pick your fears at the casting, and every day the bill offers three small real-life numbers that fit them, from warm-up to premiere. Free, no ads, no account, no internet.

## What's inside

### The casting

Eight acts, 29 fears: phone calls, speaking first, looking silly, conflict, saying no, asking for help, showing weakness, the unknown, deciding without guarantees, going places alone, making mistakes, being a beginner, sharing your work, taking praise, charging money, being seen, sport in front of people and more. Tick what's true and rate how strong it is on five lion faces, from calm to terrified.

### 1740 numbers

Sixty real things to do for every fear, on five rungs: warm-up, rehearsal, run-through, dress rehearsal, premiere. Each has a place — home, outside, work, a shop, online, on the go — and a length, from a minute to an evening. They're safe, kind to the people around you and concrete: not "be braver", but "book your doctor's appointment by phone".

### Today's bill

Three numbers a day, drawn from your fears and weighted by how strong they are. One swap a day if a number doesn't fit. Every two numbers played lift that fear a rung. Standing in a queue? "What can I do right here?" picks a number for the place you're in.

{% include figure.html path="blog/shapito-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="Tonight's bill: three numbers and one swap." %}

### Drum roll, then truth

Before a number you guess how scary it will be. A drum roll counts you in, a stopwatch runs while you do it, and afterwards you mark how it really was. The applause meter fills with the gap. After a couple of weeks the log shows the fear's average lie — in the demo circus it overstates by almost three points out of ten.

{% include figure.html path="blog/shapito-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="The lion tamer's warm-up: decline the bag at the checkout." %}

### A bow counts

Didn't work out? Tap "I fell" and take a bow. Falling is part of the circus; the number comes back when you're ready, and two falls in a row step the level back down.

### The troupe steps out

Every act starts behind the curtain. As stars pile up and the fear weakens, the performer walks out into the light: the lion goes from a roar to sitting on its drum to lying down to be stroked, the tightrope comes down, a catcher appears on the second trapeze, the stands fill and new wagons pull into the yard.

### The safety net and the posters

After a heavy number the next day is a rest with one light turn — growth needs recovery, not pressure. A five-star premiere prints a poster with your name and a line about what you learned. And now and then the circus director wires an urgent telegram with a surprise number.

## The ladder

| Level | What it is | For example |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up** | A taste of the fear, right now | Call a friend instead of texting |
| **Rehearsal** | A small real step | Book an appointment by phone |
| **Run-through** | Takes some nerve | Sort out a tricky question with the bank |
| **Dress rehearsal** | Stakes that matter | A cold call to offer working together |
| **Premiere** | Avoided for years | Call someone you've avoided out of awkwardness |

## Good to know

**Is this therapy?**

No. It borrows the idea of gradual exposure — small steps, often, with a way back — but it's a game for everyday courage, not treatment. If fear stops you living, talk to a professional.

**Do I have to do all three every day?**

No. One is a good day. The next number is always one tap away under the gold "Go" button.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9.0 (API 28) or newer. No Google services needed.
- **Permissions:** notifications only, for one reminder a day, and only if nothing has been played yet. No internet, no contacts, no account.
- **License:** MIT

1. Download the APK on your phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Go through the casting, then tap "Into the ring" on your first number.
