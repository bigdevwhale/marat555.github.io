Workout logs are useful and dull: a list of numbers you stop opening after a month. Pumpling keeps the useful part – every set, weight and run – and adds a small team of pets in a basement gym. While you train, they train: the leader presses a tiny barbell, the two beside it cheer. Every set feeds them, and they grow on rest days, like real muscles.

It's free, with no ads and no account. The only thing that leaves the phone is your town's coordinates, so the gym window can show the real sky. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### The gym

The home screen is a cosy basement gym with brick walls and a small window under the ceiling. The window shows the time of day and the weather in your town: a blue sky, clouds, rain, stars at night. Your team stands on the mat – the leader in the middle, two companions either side. Below are the leader's growth and the week: how many workouts so far against your goal.

### A workout

Start from a routine or from scratch – the app always asks first, so a stray tap never starts one. Each exercise shows what you did last time and a nudge to beat it ("try 62.5 × 8"), and a small star tells you in advance that a set will be a record. Tick a set and the rest timer starts, with a countdown in the notification. Any kind of exercise fits: weight and reps, bodyweight, assisted pull-ups, planks, runs with distance, or just minutes of yoga.

There are 140 exercises with search and filters, and you can add your own – pick its muscles on a body map, even add your own equipment.

{% include figure.html path="blog/pumpling-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="The log: workouts by week and what each one trained." %}

### Routines

33 ready routines for the gym, home and outdoors, from beginner to advanced: full body, push/pull/legs, single muscle groups, bodyweight at home, the yard bars, a 5 km run. You can look inside before taking one. On the first day a short survey – where you train, what equipment you have, your goals and level – suggests a couple to start with.

### Twenty pets

Each pet has an ability. The bullock loves legs, the otter cardio, the octopus pulls, the owl evening workouts, the phoenix a comeback after a break. Three are on the team, and their abilities add up. Eggs turn up from the way you train – a hundred pull-ups, an hour of planks, a long run – and a grown pet takes a build from what it was raised on: powerhouse, marathoner, athlete, gymnast or all-rounder.

{% include figure.html path="blog/pumpling-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="The team and the pets resting at home." %}

### Stats

A lot of them, but calm: the month at a glance, a few highlights, and sections to open when you want more. Muscles and the push/pull balance, sets per muscle each week, every exercise's progress and best weight per rep count, best running times, strength standards and DOTS, weight and measurements with before-and-after photos, and a year in review you swipe like stories.

### Cards to share

Fourteen kinds: a workout, a record, the week, month and year, a pet, your gym, the bestiary, an exercise's progress, muscles and more. Feed or story size, six backgrounds, and weights can be hidden if you'd rather not show them.

## Good to know

**Do I need a gym?**

No. There are routines for home with or without dumbbells, for the yard bars and for running, and any exercise can be added.

**What if I miss a day?**

Nothing bad: the pets grow on rest days. The week counts, not every day, and sick leave keeps your streaks while you're ill.

**It's the first version. What if something breaks?**

Please tell me: Settings → More has buttons to report a bug or suggest an idea through the StillCraft bot.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** internet only, for the weather in the gym window; notifications for the rest timer. No account, no ads, no analytics.
- **License:** MIT. Fonts: Nunito, Inter and Press Start 2P (OFL). Weather by Open-Meteo and MET Norway.

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Pick your first egg, answer four questions and take a routine.
