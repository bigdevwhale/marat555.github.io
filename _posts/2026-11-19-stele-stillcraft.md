Spaced repetition works, but flashcard apps feel like homework. Stele turns what you want to remember into an archaeology museum that you excavate and restore.

Words of a language, terms, formulas, dates, names, quotations: everything you want to keep is a find. Dust settles on it along your forgetting curve, and you brush it off by recalling it. A find memory holds longer goes from a dusty shard to clean clay, then painted, then on show. Finds on one theme glue into a vessel with gold seams. Nothing is built in: you fill the museum. Free, no ads, no account, no network.

## What's inside

### Anything worth keeping

A dig for each collection: a language, anatomy, work terms, capitals. A find is a pair, a term, a phrase with [gaps] or a question, with your own sentence, an association and a picture. Add it by voice, from text selected in any app, from the quick-settings tile, as a list, or from CSV and Anki decks.

### The brush and the dust

Each find fades along its own forgetting curve (FSRS). Recall it first, typed or in your head; the brush sweeps the dust off the answer. Pairs are asked both ways and, in a language dig, by ear.

{% include figure.html path="blog/stele-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="The museum. The temple front grows hall by hall." %}

### Chisel strikes and the workshop

Before you look, stake one to three chisel strikes. Sure and wrong cracks the find, and it waits in the workshop; the restorer's eye shows how well your confidence matches what you know.

### Vessels mended in gold

Finds on one theme are shards of an amphora, a krater, a mask, a coin. Missing while in the ground, dusty as shards, painted once restored; when all are clean the vessel stands whole, its seams in gold.

{% include figure.html path="blog/stele-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="Cleaning. Stake, then the brush sweeps the dust." %}

### The test pit and the sealed door

Every day a small test pit of finds you haven't seen for longest, with one rare find. Once a month the sealed door: twelve questions from every dig, and three in four right opens a new hall.

### Forgeries and real texts

Tell the genuine find from look-alikes and the ones you confuse. Paste or share any text to see how much of it you know, confirm the finds met in it, and take its new words into a dig.

## The learning science inside

| Technique | In Stele |
|---|---|
| **Retrieval and spacing** | Cleanings scheduled by FSRS-4.5, at the retention you choose |
| **Varied retrieval** | Front to back, back to front, by ear, gaps in a phrase |
| **Generation effect** | A sentence of your own with each find; typed answers checked |
| **Metacognition, hypercorrection** | Chisel strikes before looking, the workshop, the restorer's eye |
| **Discrimination** | Forgeries built from the finds you confuse |
| **Chunking, elaboration** | Vessels by theme; links: kin, confused with, opposite |
| **Interleaved testing** | The sealed door: twelve questions from every dig |
| **Transfer** | Sift a text: finds confirmed in real sentences |

## Good to know

**Is it only for languages?**

No. A find is anything short you want to know: a word, a term and its meaning, a formula, a date, a capital, a quotation with gaps. A dig can be set to a language so the phone speaks its words, but it doesn't have to be.

**Can I bring my Anki decks?**

Yes: import an .apkg (newer zstd packages too) and it becomes a dig. The first field is the front, the second the back; cloze notes become phrases with gaps; pictures come along from older packages. A CSV or plain-text export works as well.

**What happens if I skip days?**

Nothing breaks: there are no streaks. Dust settles on what's due, and the next cleaning starts with the dustiest finds. New finds come out of the ground a few a day, as many as you set.

**What if I change phones?**

Settings → Backup saves the whole museum, pictures included, to one JSON file. Restore it on the new phone.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer. No Google services needed.
- **Permissions:** Notifications for the evening bell. Voice, pronunciation and pictures go through the phone's own recogniser, speech and camera. No internet.
- **License:** MIT

1. Download the APK on your phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Start a dig, add a few finds (or import an Anki deck) and clean them for the first time.
