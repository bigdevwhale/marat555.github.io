Study apps tend to be either a bare timer or a spreadsheet of grades, and neither makes you want to sit down and study. Inkling puts you in an old academy of magic instead. You live in a room at the top of a tower, every subject you take belongs to one of the academy's fourteen schools – calculus to Numerology, programming to Artificing, languages to Runes, anatomy to Healing – and while you study, the creatures in your tower take in what you learn and grow at night. It works at school, at college, at university and for studying on your own.

It's free, with no ads and no account. The only thing that leaves the phone is your town's coordinates, so the arched window can show the real sky over the academy. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### The candle

The main button lights a candle: a timed lesson for a subject, and if you like for a particular course, task or exam. Put the phone face down and the circle closes. Pick it up or leave the app and the flame flickers – that's counted, never punished. When the candle burns down you write a line about what you did and what stuck, and then comes a break; the cat likes it when you really take one.

### The academy

Name your subjects the way you call them and each finds its school of magic. The week is set up once: bells, odd and even weeks, terms and holidays. After each class the tower asks whether you were there and what was set; you can photograph the homework off the board and it's due at the next class of that subject. Schoolchildren get a hob who helps pack tomorrow's satchel in the evening. During lessons the app stays quiet.

{% include figure.html path="blog/inkling-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="The academy: the week's timetable and attendance." %}

Tasks run from simple homework to labs that are done, handed in and defended, and coursework or a thesis split into stages that slowly warm a dragon's egg. Trials cover exams, credits, tests and state exams: paste the tickets as a list, mark how well you know each, and let the sphinx draw one at random. Practice papers give a predicted score. Marks work in any scale, with weights, the term mark as it stands and «two more top marks for a top mark».

### The grimoire and the familiar

Whatever you learned becomes a spell in the grimoire, and the sphinx asks about it after one, three, seven, twenty-one and sixty days, until it's yours. «Teach the familiar» is the Feynman technique: explain a topic in your own words, typed or aloud, while a raven keeps asking why, for an example, and to put it simpler. Questions you can't answer yet go into a mimic's chest until you find out.

{% include figure.html path="blog/inkling-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="The grimoire: what you learned, by subject." %}

### 24 creatures

Apart from the inkling you start with, every creature is found by the way you study: the salamander for an hour without distractions, the gargoyle for classes attended, the sphinx for spells recalled, the phoenix for a trial passed. Each has six stats and gets a speciality from the school it was raised on. Three live in the tower and double the mana for their thing; mana buys furniture and surprise eggs at the academy fair. There is also your own wand, made of your study: the wood from your main school, the core from your leader, carvings for every finished course.

{% include figure.html path="blog/inkling-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="Creatures: three in the tower and the collection of 24." %}

### The chronicle

Hours by week and month, a calendar, the star of schools, hours against credits, the memory curve, attendance and deadlines, the hours you study best, and a year in the academy at the end. Eleven kinds of share cards, from a week to your wand.

{% include figure.html path="blog/inkling-post/shot-4.jpg" caption="The chronicle: hours, the calendar and the star of schools." %}

## Good to know

**Does it work at school?**

Yes. In school mode there are lessons, homework, tomorrow's satchel, terms and state exams. In university mode there are classes, labs, coursework, the session and a record book.

**Are there flashcards?**

No – Stele and Scriptor are for that. Inkling has the grimoire: short notes on what you understood, and a sphinx that asks about them now and then.

**It's the first version. What if something breaks?**

Please tell me: Settings → StillCraft has buttons to report a bug or suggest an idea through the StillCraft bot.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** internet only for the weather in the window; notifications for the candle and reminders. The phone's own camera opens only when you photograph the board. No account, no ads, no analytics.
- **License:** MIT. Fonts: Lora, Nunito and Inter (OFL). Weather by Open-Meteo and MET Norway.

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Say where you study and add your subjects.
