My work tasks used to live in three places at once: a notes app, the calendar and my head. Deskling puts them in one small office. A Secretary in round glasses writes down what you tell her – «call the client on Friday at 3pm», «report by the 15th», «the appraisal, remind me in March» – and works out the date, the time and the folder herself. Every folder, a client or a project, gets its own clerk: it works while you time your work and grows with every task done.

It's free, with no ads, no account and no internet, and everything stays on your phone. This is the first version.

## What's inside

### One task at a time

The Today screen shows one card on the desk: the task to do now, with the clerk of its folder behind it. Stamp it done and the next card comes up; swipe it left and it goes under the pile. The day starts with the hardest task, the one you'd rather put off.

### Write it the way you say it

A new task opens the same card as an old one. Type or say a plain phrase, and the lines fill in as you go: when, how long, which folder. The rest is a question per line – how important, remind me, where to do it, who I'm waiting on.

{% include figure.html path="blog/deskling-post/shot-1.jpg" caption="A new task: the phrase already sorted into when, how long and where." %}

### All the lists, in tabs

Inbox, today, upcoming, anytime, waiting for, put off and someday sit in tabs, with the folders' clerks as a filter on top. There are other views too: by importance (the Eisenhower matrix), a kanban board and a month.

{% include figure.html path="blog/deskling-post/shot-2.jpg" caption="The lists as tabs and a filter by folder." %}

### Honest free hours

Before any task, the day loses its sleep, meals, the commute, rest and calendar meetings. What is left is the time you can actually plan, and if the plan doesn't fit, the morning briefing says so and offers to move the extra to days with room.

### Time by client and stage

Start the clock on a task with one tap; it keeps running in the notification shade with the app closed. A dial shows where the week went, folder by folder, an hourly rate turns hours into money, and the whole period exports as a CSV table for an invoice.

{% include figure.html path="blog/deskling-post/shot-3.jpg" caption="Where the week went, by client." %}

### Briefing, review and an office that grows

In the morning, a short briefing sorts the inbox one task at a time and picks the hardest of the day; on Friday, a review goes over the week, the overdue tails and who you're waiting on. Six staff posts and eighteen kinds of folder clerks rise from intern to director, and paperclips buy furniture for an office that grows from a booth to a department.

## Get it

- **Requires:** Android 9 (API 28) or newer.
- **Permissions:** notifications for reminders and the stopwatch; the calendar only if you ask to take meetings out of your free hours. No ads, no account, no analytics, no internet.
- **License:** MIT.

1. Download the APK on the phone.
1. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
1. Give the Secretary a name and write down your first task.
